Services

How I can help

I offer art therapy for children and young people, working alongside families and schools so that each child has a consistent, safe space to explore what's going on for them.

  • WhoChildren and young people aged 6 to 18
  • WhereSchools across NW London & Hertfordshire
  • How oftenWeekly, 50 minute sessions

Children & young people

One-to-one art therapy

Sessions give a child time with a trained therapist, in the same room at the same time each week. They can draw, paint, model with clay, play or talk. They lead, and I follow, notice and respond.

Children don't need to be good at art, or even to enjoy it at first. Nothing they make is judged or marked. It's a way of expressing feelings and experiences that can be hard to say out loud.

Who I help

Children

  • Early intervention support for children in their primary years
  • Using art therapy to help children express feelings when words are still developing
  • Building emotional regulation and confidence through play and art-making
  • Supporting children from around age 6 and above

Adolescents

  • A safe, confidential space to express big feelings that are often kept inside
  • Support with stress around school, exams and friendships
  • Using art and creativity to explore identity and self-esteem
  • Helping teenagers make sense of who they are during a very intense stage of life

Families

  • Therapy is centred on the child, with support for the whole family
  • Parent reflection sessions to explore family dynamics and how they may be affecting your child
  • Signposting to classes, support groups and workshops that may benefit your family
  • Aiming to help families grow in understanding, connection and happiness

Sessions and fees

  • Assessment, 60 minutes: £90. A consultation with you, the parents or guardians. I'll ask about your child's background and the challenges they're facing, and together we'll set some achievable aims for therapy.
  • Individual art therapy sessions, 50 minutes, weekly: £85 per session. Held in school, in a consistent room each week.
  • Regular and end reviews, 60 minutes: £90. After 6 weeks, and then every half term, we review your child's progress and challenges together. If you or your child decide not to continue, an end-of-therapy review is completed.
  • Written reports for EHCP or school purposes are charged separately. Happy to discuss on request.

Art materials and artwork

I provide all the art materials. Each child has their own folder, and their artwork stays safely with me until therapy ends. Keeping the artwork in the room is an important part of the art therapy process.

Schools

Art therapy in your school

Having a therapist on site means pupils can be seen consistently, in a setting they know, without time out of school for appointments.

What's included

  • A clear referral and assessment process, agreed with your team
  • Weekly one-to-one sessions in a private, consistent room
  • Safeguarding and confidentiality in line with your school's policies
  • Reviews after 6 weeks and every half term, and an end-of-therapy review when the work finishes
  • Written reports for EHCP or school purposes, for an additional fee

Pricing for schools

As part of your school's wellbeing provision, I charge a day rate of £400 to see 5 pupils each week.

Schools can also get in touch about a particular pupil and family who might benefit from art therapy. If the school is funding the therapy, a member of staff can join the assessment call so we can agree this together.
