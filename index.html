Meet Shoshie

Another way to say it.

Growing up in a world of neurodiversity taught me patience, kindness and understanding from an early age. I went on to teach neurodivergent children for six years, trained as an art therapist, and have since supported many children, adolescents and their parents at Norwood.

Some things are hard to put into words, especially for children. Art therapy offers another way in: through paint, clay, drawing and play, alongside talking when they're ready. There's no right way to do it and nothing they have to be good at.
