Privacy

Privacy notice

How this website and the enquiry form use your information.

Draft for you to check. This covers the website and enquiries only. Your client records need their own privacy notice as part of your practice paperwork.

Who I am

This website is run by Shoshie Ross, trading as Shoshie Ross Art Therapy. I'm responsible for the information you send through this site (the "data controller"). I'm registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), registration number [ICO number].

You can contact me about anything in this notice at shoshie@shoshierossat.com.

What I collect

If you send an enquiry, I receive the details you enter: your name, email address, your phone number if you give it, who you are (for example a parent or a school), what you'd like to arrange, the age range of the child if you give it, and your message.

Please don't include a child's full name or detailed personal or health information in the form. We can talk about that directly.

How I use it

I use your details only to reply to your enquiry and, if you'd like to go ahead, to arrange a call, an assessment or a review. I don't use them for marketing, and I never sell or share them.

I rely on the fact that you've asked me to contact you, and on my legitimate interest in replying to enquiries about my work.

Who else handles it

  • Web3Forms passes your enquiry from the form to my email inbox.
  • Microsoft 365, provided through GoDaddy, hosts my email.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like most web hosts, it may keep short-term technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces on this site. When a page loads, your browser requests them from Google, which receives your IP address.

How long I keep it

If we don't go on to work together, I delete your enquiry within 6 months. If we do, it becomes part of your client record, which is covered by my client privacy notice.

Cookies

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or any other tracking.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information I hold about you, ask me to correct or delete it, or object to how I use it. Email me and I'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office or call them on 0303 123 1113.
